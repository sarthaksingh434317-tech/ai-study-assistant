AI Study Assistant Guide

1. AI Study Chat

Have an open conversation about any homework problem, concept, or essay prompt with smart context memory.

Generate Cornell notes or comprehensive exam sheets. Download as Markdown or print for revision.

3. Topic Summarizer

Paste dense textbook chapters or research papers to extract quick summaries and bullet points.

4. MCQ Generator

Create multiple-choice questions with full answer keys and rationales to prepare for tests.

5. Practice Quiz

Take an interactive exam with immediate feedback, scoring, and step-by-step solutions.

6. Simple Explanation

Demystify complex topics using everyday analogies and simple language tailored for beginners.
